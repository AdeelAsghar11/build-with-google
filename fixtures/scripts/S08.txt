Assignment Submission: Overfitting

Overfitting is when a machine learning model learns the training data too specifically. Because of this, it cannot generalize properly to new examples.

It can be detected by looking at the gap between the training score and the validation score. If train is 99% and validation is 70%, that is overfitting.

Ways to reduce it:
1. Data Augmentation: generating more diverse samples to train the model.
2. Regularization like L2 weight penalty.

Example:
If you train a neural network to recognize cats, but all cat photos in your training set have grass in the background, the network might predict cat whenever it sees grass.
